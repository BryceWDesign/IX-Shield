# IX-Shield Glossary

## Purpose
This glossary defines the core terms used throughout the IX-Shield repository.

The definitions are written for concept-stage repository clarity.
They are not intended to replace formal standards documents or mission-specific terminology controls.

---

## A

### Areal Density
Mass per unit area of a shielding or structural stack.
In IX-Shield, areal density is used as a concept-screening metric for comparing stack options.

---

## B

### Baseline Transit Shell
The general crew-shell protection concept used during nominal habitation and transit operations.

### Bill of Materials
A structured list of concept-stage materials, subsystems, and constituent items used to describe the architecture and support review.

---

## C

### Concept Screening
Early-stage evaluation used to compare architectures, stacks, or zone allocations using bounded assumptions and simplified metrics.

### Crew-Core Logic
The IX-Shield design principle that crew protection depends strongly on how crew are positioned relative to surrounding protective mass.

---

## D

### Deep-Space Transit
Mission conditions outside low Earth orbit where crews are exposed to a mixed radiation environment with limited or absent geomagnetic protection.

### Dense High-Z Material
A material with relatively high atomic number used in some shielding contexts.
In IX-Shield, such materials are not assumed to be the default primary crew-shield answer.

---

## E

### Evaluation-Only License
The custom repository license under which IX-Shield is distributed.
It allows review and non-operational evaluation under stated conditions but does not grant unrestricted implementation rights.

---

## F

### Functional Outer Boundary
The exterior-facing boundary of the architecture.
It serves as the first physical interface with the environment but is not assumed to be the sole radiation solution.

---

## G

### Galactic Cosmic Rays
Persistent high-energy radiation present in deep-space environments.
In IX-Shield, GCR is treated as a chronic background hazard that limits how aggressively passive shielding claims can be made.

### Geometry-Aware Protection
The design principle that shielding effectiveness depends not only on nominal material thickness, but also on crew placement, surrounding mass, and local architectural layout.

---

## H

### Hydrogen-Rich Material
A material category used in IX-Shield for primary shielding roles because it aligns with a low-Z, crew-protection-oriented design philosophy.

### Habitat Zone
A local region of the vehicle or habitat used in IX-Shield for mass-allocation and crew-protection reasoning.

---

## I

### Inner Occupied Boundary
The crew-facing interior side of the conceptual stack.

### Interlayer
A layer positioned between major shielding regions that may support structural, separation, or tuned multifunctional roles.

---

## L

### Low-Z Material
A material with relatively low atomic number.
In IX-Shield, low-Z and hydrogen-rich materials are favored for the main passive shielding role.

---

## M

### Mass Allocation
The assignment of shielding or system mass to specific architectural regions.

### Multifunctional Shielding
A design approach in which shielding mass also contributes to other roles such as structure, storage, protection, or operational sheltering.

---

## N

### Non-Claims
Deliberate statements that define what the repository does not assert, such as flight readiness, full protection, or operational validation.

---

## O

### Operational Shelter Logic
The combination of architecture and crew procedures that supports moving into a more protected refuge during elevated radiation conditions.

### Outer Functional Boundary
See Functional Outer Boundary.

---

## P

### Primary Shielding Layer
The main passive shielding contributor in the IX-Shield baseline concept.

### Proof of Concept
A bounded repository stage intended to show that an architecture can be framed, configured, and screened seriously without implying final validation.

---

## R

### Refuge Volume
A smaller crew-occupied zone intended to receive concentrated protection during storm-shelter operations.

### Repository Phase
The current maturity stage of the IX-Shield project as represented by the files, analyses, and documentation in the repository.

---

## S

### Secondary Radiation
Radiation generated when incoming radiation interacts with shielding materials, structure, internal mass, or tissue.

### Shelter Enhancement
The local increase in shielding mass around a smaller crew refuge zone to improve response to event-driven radiation exposure.

### Solar Particle Event
A radiation event associated with solar activity that can drive rapid increases in crew exposure risk over shorter time windows than chronic background exposure.

### Stand-Off Region
A separated intermediate region used in the conceptual cross section to support multifunctional spacing and protection logic.

### Storm Shelter
A concentrated refuge region intended to provide greater local protection than the baseline transit shell during elevated radiation conditions.

---

## T

### Threat Model
The bounded description of environment classes and radiation categories used to drive IX-Shield architecture choices.

### Transit Shell
See Baseline Transit Shell.

---

## W

### Water Equivalent
A concept-stage way of expressing protection or added shielding mass relative to an equivalent depth or shielding effect benchmarked against water.

---

## Z

### Zone Mass Budget
A structured accounting of how much mass is assigned to different architectural protection zones in the concept.

### Zone Reference Configuration
A machine-readable definition of the habitat or vehicle zones used for screening mass-allocation logic.

---

## Summary
The IX-Shield glossary exists to keep repository language stable, bounded, and reviewable.
